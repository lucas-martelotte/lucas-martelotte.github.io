\section{Monoids}

Recall from~\cref{thm:ltopab_is_monoidal} that the category $(\textbf{LTopAb}, \otimes, \mathbb{Z})$ of linearly topologized abelian groups (LTA's) together with the strong tensor product form a symmetric monoidal category. We define a strongly linearly topologized ring (SLTR for short) as a monoid object in $\textbf{LTopAb}$. Given a SLTR $R$, we define a strongly linearly topologized $R$-module ($R$-SLTM for short) as a $R$-module object in $\textbf{LTopAb}$.

\begin{remark} The word "strong" here serves the purpose of distinguishing our definition from the more common weaker definition in the literature, where the tensor product is instead equipped with the finest topology making the morphism $G \times H \rightarrow G \otimes H$ continuous.
\end{remark}
